**1. What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic that we have covered this quarter is Fractal Chain of Thought. Fractal chain of thought is a recursive prompting structure in which the same reasoning pattern is applied at multiple levels of a problem. We use it at the Micro, meso, and macro levels.
